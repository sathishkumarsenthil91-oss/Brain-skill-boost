Brain Boost for Android

Your existing Brain Boost account, mobile design, learning tools, and chats—in an Android app.

Download Android APKVersion 1.0.0 · Android 6 or newer · Keep Chrome updated
  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser, then tap Install.
  4. Open Brain Boost and sign in with your existing account.

The app uses your website account. Internet access is required for live features.
